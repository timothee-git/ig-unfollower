How to get your Instagram data

  1. 01
    Open Instagram settings

    Go to Settings → Your activity → Download your information

  2. 02
    Request JSON format

    Select JSON (not HTML). You'll get a download link by email in ~30 min.

  3. 03
    Find your files

    Unzip the archive. Inside connections/followers_and_following/ find all followers_*.json files and following.json.

  4. 04
    Upload & analyze

    Drop your files below. If you have multiple followers files, select them all at once.
